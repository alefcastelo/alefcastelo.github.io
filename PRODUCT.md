# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Software engineers and technical leaders (tech leads, engineering managers) who read about engineering process and architecture.
- Recruiters and hiring decision-makers evaluating Alef for roles, consulting or talks.
- Teams that may become Bidirekt users, arriving through posts about contract testing.

## Product Purpose

alefcastelo.com is the personal blog of Alef Castelo, a software engineer in Mexico City with over a decade of backend work. He writes about whatever he finds interesting, to build authority online. Success means readers finish posts, come back, subscribe to the newsletter, and leave with a clear picture of who Alef is and what he builds.

## Positioning

Writing from a practitioner who measures: posts argue from his own experience and real numbers (the first post reports review time per MR dropping from 21.8h to 3.6h with BDD). The author is also building Bidirekt, a contract-testing product, so the blog carries first-hand writing on why contract tests matter.

## Operating Context

- Posts are Markdown files in `content/posts/`, written in English, with title, description and date.
- Topics: AI and engineering process, backend and architecture, building Bidirekt, and anything else he finds interesting.
- Readers can sign in with GitHub or Google to comment and react, and subscribe to a newsletter that only collects e-mails.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- TanStack Start server-rendered on a Hostinger VPS (Dokploy), SQLite storage.
- Images are served from `public/images/<post-slug>/`.
- Bidirekt gets links from the blog and possibly a dedicated page (undecided), plus posts about it.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Alef Castelo. Photo: `public/me.jpg`.
- Profiles: GitHub https://github.com/alefcastelo, LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/alefcastelo, Bidirekt https://bidirekt.com.
- Voice: first person, direct, opinionated, backed by numbers.

## Evidence on Hand

- One published post: `content/posts/bdd-can-make-your-code-review-6x-faster.md`.
- No testimonials, talks, press or employer logos exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Reading comes first: the post is the product.
2. Every page answers "who wrote this and why trust him" without shouting.
3. Bidirekt is present and reachable, never an ad over the writing.
4. Claims stay real: no invented metrics, logos or social proof.
